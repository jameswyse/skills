# Explore design options

Explore alternatives before implementing a novel interaction or architectural decision with no established precedent.

## Create the options

- Confirm that the decision has multiple viable approaches and no established local pattern. If the constraints leave only one credible approach, explain that and stop.
- Build two or three prototypes or sketches that differ in structure or behaviour. Cosmetic variations of the same design count as one option.
- Keep exploratory artefacts separate from production behaviour unless the user asks to retain an option.

## Compare and recommend

Compare the options against the task's actual constraints, including user experience, maintainability, risk and cost where relevant. Demonstrate important differences side by side, recommend one option and explain which evidence decides the choice.

Do not use this skill for mechanical work, a bug with a known target state, or a change constrained to one viable design.
